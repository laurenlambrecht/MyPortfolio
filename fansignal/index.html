    How FanSignal works

    01 · Observe

    Load historical Google Trends-style data by date and team, on a consistent scale.

    02 · Interpret

    Compare the latest four observations with the previous four, in index points.

    03 · Recommend

    Apply transparent momentum thresholds to suggest a test and relevant validation metrics.

    FanSignal is an additional product prototype layered on the Sports Fan Engagement Dashboard research project. Uploading CSVs processes data locally in your browser. No live API, event attribution, or conversion measurement is claimed.
